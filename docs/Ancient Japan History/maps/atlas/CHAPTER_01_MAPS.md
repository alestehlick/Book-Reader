# Chapter 1 narrative atlas

Chapter 1 now uses ten scalable scenes instead of two standing plates: emerging islands, Paleolithic movement, Jomon coasts, Middle Jomon highlands, Late/Final Jomon networks, the arrival of rice, Yayoi communities, bronze and metal networks, the written itinerary through Wa, and mythic memory beside archaeological regional centers.

Every paragraph receives one relevant scene through its section assignment. The map has no surface title. The compact **Map key** explains only the marks visible in that scene, and tapping a feature gives its date, object type, evidence basis, certainty, and one restrained interpretive note.

The initial view is deliberately selective. Primary features and labels appear first; contextual places and rivers emerge during enlargement. The terrain remains quiet enough for the historical hierarchy to read immediately. Static WebP fallbacks preserve the same composition when WebGL, PMTiles byte ranges, or the network runtime is unavailable.

The editable source and publication procedure are documented in `tools/map_data/README.md`.
